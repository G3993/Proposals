The Takeoff

Live entertainment is about
to change forever.

Real-time generative AI is on an exponential curve. Two years ago, it took minutes to render a single image. Today, a world model turns speech into spatial imagination at 30 frames per second, projected onto walls and ceilings, synchronized to music. Tomorrow, performers will have creative partners that see, respond, and improvise alongside them.

The Medium

Every performance
is born live.

A poet speaks and the room becomes what they describe. A singer steps on stage alone and suddenly has a full visual band. A storyteller conjures characters that the audience can see. A DJ's set paints trails of light across every surface.

Generative art is one of a kind by definition. Each performance will never repeat. It belongs to the people in that room, on that night.
